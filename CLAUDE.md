## WHAT HA_CHAIN IS

### One-Line Description
"A peer-to-peer currency matching protocol where money never crosses borders -- the Uniswap of remittance."

### The Core Mechanism
Instead of sending money across borders (expensive, slow, regulated), we MATCH people who need opposite currencies:
- Ali in USA has USD, needs INR sent to India
- Raj in India has INR, needs USD sent to USA
- Ali's USD goes to Raj's US recipient (domestic ACH transfer)
- Raj's INR goes to Ali's India recipient (domestic UPI transfer)
- NO money crosses any border. NO FX conversion. Near-ZERO fees.

## THE THREE-LAYER LEGAL ARCHITECTURE

### Layer 1: Immutable Protocol (Cannot be regulated)
- Smart contracts deployed on blockchain (Stellar/Base)
- Matching engine, escrow, liquidity pools, obligation ledger
- Legal precedent: Van Loon v. Treasury (5th Circuit, Nov 2024) -- immutable smart contracts are NOT property, cannot be sanctioned
- DOJ policy (Aug 2025): will NOT prosecute developers of genuinely decentralized protocols

### Layer 2: Licensed Interfaces (Regulated normally)
- Apps/frontends that connect users to the protocol
- Each licensed in its jurisdiction
- EU: MiCA CASP license (1 license = 27 countries via passporting)
- US: GENIUS Act FQPSI or partner with licensed MTL
- El Salvador: BSP + DASP ($2,000, 20 days)
- Singapore: MAS sandbox (21 days)

### Layer 3: Local Payment Rails (Already regulated)
- UPI (India), SPEI (Mexico), SEPA (EU), M-Pesa (Kenya), Zaad (Somaliland), GCash (Philippines)
- Already licensed in their jurisdictions
- We connect to them via API

3. **Immutable smart contracts** -- legal protection via Tornado Cash precedent
4. **Protocol + Interface separation** -- protocol is ungovernable code, interfaces are licensed companies
5. **Matching pool model** -- money never crosses borders, near-zero fees
6. **Start in Somaliland** -- zero competition, zero regulation barriers, mobile money ready
