## THE GENIUS ACT -- IT'S LAW (Signed July 18, 2025)

The Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for U.S. Stablecoins (GENIUS) Act passed with strong bipartisan support:
- **Senate**: 68-30 (June 17, 2025)
- **House**: 308-122 (July 17, 2025)
- **Signed by President**: July 18, 2025
- **Takes full effect**: January 18, 2027, or 120 days after regulators finalize rules
- **OCC proposed rulemaking**: March 2, 2026 (comment deadline May 1, 2026)

### The Mechanism

The GENIUS Act says: "If you want to issue a stablecoin, you must back every token 1:1 with **US Treasuries (93 days or less) or cash at US banks**."

So what happens when stablecoins grow?

```
Step 1: Circle mints 1 USDC
Step 2: Circle MUST buy $1 of US Treasury bonds
Step 3: Repeat 230 billion times...
Step 4: Stablecoin issuers become the BIGGEST buyers of US debt

Current stablecoin supply:  ~$320B (already 17th largest holder of Treasuries)
Projected 2028:             ~$2 TRILLION (Standard Chartered)
Projected 2030:             ~$4 TRILLION (Citi bull case)
```

**Every stablecoin minted = forced buyer of US government debt.** The US just turned the entire crypto industry into a Treasury bond purchasing machine.

### Three Issuer Pathways

1. **Subsidiaries of insured depository institutions** -- Regulated by existing federal banking agency (OCC, FDIC, NCUA)
2. **Federally qualified nonbank payment stablecoin issuers** -- New category, regulated by the OCC
3. **State-qualified payment stablecoin issuers** -- For issuers with <$10B outstanding, under state regimes certified as "substantially similar" to federal framework

### The $10 Billion Threshold

Once a state-qualified issuer exceeds $10B in outstanding stablecoins, it must either transition to federal regime within 360 days or obtain a waiver.

### Big Tech Restriction

Public companies not predominantly in financial activities are **prohibited** from issuing stablecoins without clearance from a Stablecoin Certification Review Committee. This blocks Apple, Amazon, Google from casually entering as issuers.
