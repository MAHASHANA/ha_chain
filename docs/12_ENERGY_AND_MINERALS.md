## THE HELIUM CRISIS: The Invisible Chokepoint

### What Happened (Iran War)

Iran attacked Qatar's **Ras Laffan Industrial City** -- the world's largest LNG/helium facility:
- ~27% of world helium supply taken offline
- ~5.2 million cubic meters/month missing
- Spot helium prices: **doubled, some reports 400% surge**
- No viable alternative to helium for semiconductor manufacturing

### Why Helium Is Irreplaceable for Chips

- **Heat transfer:** Cools wafers during lithography
- **EUV lithography:** Required for extreme ultraviolet printing at smallest nodes
- **Leak detection:** Tests hermetic seals on chip packages
- Semiconductor helium demand: 6% (2015) → **21-25%** (2025) of global consumption

### Who Is Most Exposed

- **South Korea:** Samsung + SK Hynix = ~66% of global memory chips. Sourced 65% of helium from Qatar. Samsung has 6-12 week buffer.
- **TSMC (Taiwan)** and **Micron (US):** Also exposed but more diversified.

If Ras Laffan stays offline for months → memory chip prices spike → AI GPU production slows → data center buildouts delayed → cascading into blockchain infrastructure.

## THE FUNDAMENTAL PARADOX

### The US Position

Controls: reserve currency, SWIFT, dollar clearing, digital dollar infrastructure, stablecoin regulation, military, technology design (Nvidia, Apple, Google)

### China's Position

Controls: 90% rare earth processing, 98% gallium, 80%+ cobalt/germanium/graphite, 95% manganese/cathode precursors, manufacturing base for most electronics

### Who Has Real Leverage?

**The US is building a digital financial empire on a physical foundation it does not control.**

Every server running USDC contains chips made with Chinese-processed rare earths. Every data center depends on equipment whose minerals China refines. Even ASML's EUV machines use Chinese-processed rare earth components.

The dollar's power is real but **abstract** (trust, institutions, inertia).
China's resource power is **physical and concrete** (you cannot synthesize gallium).

### Three Scenarios

**Scenario A -- Stable Cold War:** Mutual deterrence. US threatens financial exclusion, China threatens resource cutoffs. Neither pushes to breaking point.

**Scenario B -- Full Decoupling:** China halts rare earth exports permanently. US faces 5-15 years of severe tech disruption while building alternatives. Both sides lose, pain is asymmetric.

**Scenario C -- BRICS Resource Leverage:** China uses minerals to accelerate de-dollarization. "Want our minerals? Pay in yuan." Already happening with Iran oil-for-yuan.
